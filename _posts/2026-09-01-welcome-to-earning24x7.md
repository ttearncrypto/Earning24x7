## Terminology: The Words You Need to Know

The internet money-making world is full of acronyms. It sounds like alphabet soup at first. Here are the terms we use most, explained in plain English.

Here's the whole glossary as a mind map:

- **Online Money-Making Terms**
  - **Affiliate Marketing**: affiliate program, affiliate network, commission
  - **Pay Models**: CPC (Cost Per Click), CPM (Cost Per Mille), CPA (Cost Per Action), PTC (Paid To Click)
  - **Ad Networks**: AdSense, high CPM networks, AdSense alternatives
  - **Tools**: URL shortener, link shortener
  - **Income**: ad revenue, commissions, CPA payouts

### What is an Affiliate Program?

An affiliate program is a partnership where you promote a product or service for a merchant. When someone buys through your unique link, you earn a commission.

You don't create the product. You don't handle shipping or support. You just bring customers, and you get paid for it.

### What is Affiliate Marketing?

Affiliate marketing is the process of earning that commission. You choose a niche, create content, share affiliate links, and earn when your audience takes action.

It works on blogs, YouTube, email lists, social media, and more.

### What is an Affiliate Network?

An affiliate network is a middleman that connects merchants with affiliates. It hosts the offers, tracks your clicks, and handles your payouts.

Networks make it easy to join many programs through one dashboard instead of signing up with every merchant separately.

### What is a CPA Affiliate Network?

CPA stands for Cost Per Action. A CPA network pays you for a specific action, not just a click.

That action could be a sale, a form fill, a free trial, or an app install. CPA offers often pay well because the merchant gets a committed user, not just a visitor.

### What is CPC?

CPC stands for Cost Per Click. The advertiser pays you each time someone clicks an ad. Google AdSense is the classic example; you earn whenever a visitor clicks a display ad on your site. [Google's AdSense help center](https://support.google.com/adsense/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"} is a good place to learn the official rules.

### What is CPM?

CPM stands for Cost Per Mille, which means cost per thousand impressions. You get paid for every 1,000 times an ad is shown, whether anyone clicks or not.

High CPM networks pay more per 1,000 views, which is why site owners hunt for them when their traffic is high but click-through is low.

### What is PTC?

PTC stands for Paid To Click. PTC sites pay you to view or click ads for a set number of seconds. It's a simple, low-effort way to earn small amounts, though it rarely replaces a real income.

### What is CPA Marketing?

CPA marketing is promoting CPA offers to earn the per-action payout. It's a favorite of digital marketers because you get paid for results, not just traffic.

### What is a URL Shortener and Link Shortener?

A URL shortener turns a long link into a short one and can pay you when people use your shortened links. It's a quick way to monetize links you share on social media, in bios, or in messages.
