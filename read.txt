## 📊 Network Evaluation Criteria
The blog assesses and compiles ratings for various affiliate networks based on real affiliate feedback across five distinct metrics:

   1. Offers: The quantity and quality of available campaigns.
   2. Payout: The reliability, frequency, and amount of distributions.
   3. Tracking: The technical accuracy of link clicks and conversions.
   4. Support: The responsiveness of dedicated affiliate managers.
   5. Overall Rating: A synthesized score pulling from aggregated data on aggregators like Affpaying.
